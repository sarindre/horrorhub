**Subject:** Question about API terms: a free personal app, optional tips, and a possible paid release

Hello,

I'm the developer of HorrorHub (https://github.com/sarindre/horrorhub), a personal horror movie library and planning app. It runs on the user's own device (web, installable app, and a desktop app) and stores everything locally. There are no accounts and no server of mine. It uses the TMDB API to look up film details, posters and keywords. Each user creates their own free TMDB API key and pastes it into the app; the key stays on their device and is only sent to TMDB. The app shows the TMDB logo and the required attribution statement.

I'd like to understand what is allowed before I distribute it more widely. I have three scenarios and would be grateful for your guidance on each:

1. **Free and non-commercial.** The app is free, with no ads and no purchases, and users bring their own key. Is it fine to distribute this freely, including as a free listing on a game/software store such as Steam (where I would pay a fee as the publisher but charge users nothing)?

2. **Optional tips.** The app stays free and nothing is gated, but I would like to add a link where people can voluntarily support the project (for example through a donations page). Does that count as commercial use under your terms?

3. **A paid release.** If I later sell the app, I understand I'd need a written agreement. Could you tell me what a commercial agreement involves and what it costs for a small project? In particular:
   - Is the commercial plan I've seen described (about $149/month for under $1M revenue) accurate?
   - Does it include use of poster and image files?
   - Could the key be shipped inside a desktop app, or must it be held behind a small server of mine?
   - Is it acceptable for each customer to use their own personal key in a paid app, or would I need my own commercial key?

Two technical questions as well:

4. **Local storage of data.** To work offline, the app keeps some TMDB responses on the user's device (a day's worth of recommendation lists, and up to a few hundred poster images the user has viewed). Is that acceptable?
5. **Attribution.** Is the current attribution (logo in the footer, plus "This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.") sufficient, or do you require anything more?

Thank you for the work you do on TMDB; the app wouldn't exist without it.
